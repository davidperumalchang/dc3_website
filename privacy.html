Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may collect certain personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide to us. For example, when you complete our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), and the contents of your message. The information that we request will be retained and used as described in this Privacy Notice.

This information is used to analyse website traffic, monitor performance, and improve the user experience of the Service.

Log Data

We want to inform you that whenever you use our Service, in the case of an error or through analytics tools, we may collect data and information on your device called Log Data.

This Log Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, browser type, pages visited, the configuration of the Service when utilising it, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. These are sent to your browser from the websites that you visit and are stored on your device.

This Service uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics cookies, to collect information, analyse traffic, and improve the user experience.

You have the option to accept or refuse cookies through your browser settings. However, if you choose to refuse cookies, some portions of the Service may not function properly.

Some pages of our Service display content from third parties or retrieve information from their services. In particular, we use Google services to power parts of the Service:

When these features load, your browser communicates directly with the relevant third party, which may receive information such as your IP address and details about your device and browser.
