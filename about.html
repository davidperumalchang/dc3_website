Who We Are

A great church for every nation.

Destiny C3 is a movement of churches and a family of believers passionate about Jesus, people, and the nations. We exist to bring Heaven to Earth — one life, one city, one nation at a time.

What We Believe

The convictions that shape us.

We live by our convictions — the core values that make us who we are. The Bible is our guide to life, not only because it is good, but because it is true.

  1. 01

    There is one God: God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Spirit.

  2. 02

    In the deity of our Lord Jesus Christ the Son of God; we believe in His virgin birth, in His sinless life, in His miracles, in His vicarious and atoning death, in His bodily resurrection, in His ascension to the right hand of the Father, His constant intercession and in His imminent return.

  3. 03

    In the person and work of the Holy Spirit with His fruit and gifts available in the Church.

  4. 04

    The Bible as the living word of God — infallible, authoritative and everlasting, and the foundation of all Christian doctrine and faith.

  5. 05

    In the existence of an evil spiritual being — the devil.

  6. 06

    In the spiritually lost condition of all people and the essential need for the "new birth" by faith in Jesus Christ.

  7. 07

    In the baptism of the Holy Spirit as a gift available to believers, with the normal evidence of speaking in other tongues.

  8. 08

    In the sacraments of the Lord's Supper and baptism by full immersion in water for all believers.

  9. 09

    In the resurrection of both the saved and the lost, the one to everlasting life and the other to everlasting separation from God.

  10. 10

    In the church being the body of Christ, and each member being an active part of a local church, fulfilling the Great Commission.

  11. 11

    Marriage was instituted by God, ratified by Jesus, and is exclusively between a man and a woman. It is a picture of Christ and His church.

  12. 12

    Sex is a gift from God for procreation and unity, and it is only appropriate within and designed for marriage.
