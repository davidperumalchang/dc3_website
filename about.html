Who We Are

A great church for every nation.

Destiny C3 is a movement of churches and a family of believers passionate about Jesus, people, and the nations. We exist to bring Heaven to Earth — one life, one city, one nation at a time.

Our Vision

We dream of a great church for every nation.

A house of worship. A house of prayer. A house of family. Our vision is to see a great church planted in every city, in every nation — a community where every person can encounter the love of Jesus, discover their purpose, and become everything God has destined them to be.

We believe the local church is the hope of the world. From our home in Malaysia, we're partnering with God to build a global family that transforms lives, communities, and nations.

Our Story

From one city to many nations.

Beginning

A vision birthed in prayer

Destiny C3 began with a small group of believers, a big dream, and a deep conviction that the local church could change the world.

Growth

Roots across Malaysia

From Kuala Lumpur, the church multiplied across Malaysia — Ipoh, Penang, Johor Bahru, Kuching, Kota Kinabalu. Lives transformed. Families restored. Generations awakened.

Expansion

A movement across Asia

What began locally became regional. Today, Destiny C3 churches are planted across Asia — India, Philippines, Thailand and beyond — each one a house for the hurting, the seeking, and the called.

Today

A family for every nation

We are still dreaming. Still planting. Still believing that every nation needs a great church — and that the best is yet to come.

What We Believe

The values that shape us.

We are a Bible-believing, Spirit-empowered, people-loving church. These are the convictions that anchor everything we do.

The Bible

The Bible is the inspired Word of God — our final authority for faith, life, and practice.

Jesus Christ

We believe in one God, eternally existent as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Jesus is Lord and Saviour.

The Holy Spirit

We are empowered by the Holy Spirit to live for God, love people, and reach the world.

The Church

The local church is God's plan A — a family, a body, and a force for good in the earth.

The Mission

We are sent — to preach the gospel, make disciples, and disciple nations until Jesus returns.

The Future

Jesus is coming again. Until that day, we live with hope, urgency, and joy — for the best is yet to come.
