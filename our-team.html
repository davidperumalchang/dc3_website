Our Team

Meet the team behind Destiny C3.

Pastors and leaders across Malaysia, India, Thailand and Cambodia — building God's house and raising God's people.

Lead Pastors

Pastors Clarance & Deborah

Pastors Clarance & Deborah lead the Destiny C3 family from our home base in Subang Jaya, Malaysia. With a heart for the local church and a vision for the nations, they pastor a growing movement that spans countries, cultures, and generations.

Their passion is simple: to see lives transformed by the love of Jesus, families restored, and a great church planted in every nation.
